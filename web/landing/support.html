Frequently Asked Questions

How do I invite someone to my household?

Open the app, go to Profile → Household, then tap Invite Member. They'll receive a link to join. If they already have the app installed, it will open directly; otherwise they'll be prompted to download it first.

Can I switch between household modes?

Yes. Sivena supports multiple household modes — Couple, Family, Parenting, and more. You can switch modes in Profile → Household Mode. Each mode surfaces the most relevant features for your situation.

My data isn't syncing across devices. What should I do?

First, ensure both devices are connected to the internet and you're signed in with the same account. Try pulling down on the main screen to force a refresh. If the problem persists, sign out and sign back in, or contact support.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple. On your iPhone or iPad, go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions, find Sivena, and tap Cancel Subscription. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data. Alternatively, you can request deletion via our account deletion page.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your household data is only visible to members you explicitly invite. We do not sell your data or share it with third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

We'd love to hear from you. Email support@sivena.app with as much detail as possible — screenshots are always helpful.
