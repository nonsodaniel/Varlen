✦ FAQ

Common questions

Is Sivena free to download? +
Yes — Sivena is free to download and use. The free plan gives you access to tasks, calendar, basic expense splitting, and more. Pro and Family plans unlock advanced features like meal planning, savings goals, wellness trends, and unlimited lists.
What platforms is Sivena available on? +
Sivena is live on iOS — download it now from the App Store. Android support is actively in development and coming soon. Sign up and we'll notify you when it's ready.
Can couples or families share one account? +
Each person creates their own Sivena account, then joins a shared "circle" — your household. Everything inside the circle (calendar, tasks, expenses, lists) is synced in real time. The Family plan supports up to 6 members per circle.
Can I try premium features before subscribing? +
Yes! Every premium feature comes with 3 free preview uses so you can experience the full app before deciding. No credit card required to try.
How does expense splitting work? +
Add an expense, choose who was involved, and Sivena calculates who owes what. You can split evenly or by custom amounts. When you're ready, use the "Settle Up" feature to mark balances as cleared.
Is my data private and secure? +
Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Private content (like your personal journal) is only visible to you. Shared content is only visible to members of your circle. We never sell your data.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? +
Yes. Subscriptions are managed through the App Store and can be cancelled at any time. You'll retain access until the end of the current billing period. No hidden fees.
