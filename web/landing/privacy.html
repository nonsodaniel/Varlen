Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

1 Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, including your name, email address, and household data you create within the app — tasks, events, expenses, memories, and more.

2 How We Use Your Information

Your data is used exclusively to provide and improve the Sivena service. We use it to:

  • Sync your household data across devices
  • Send you relevant notifications
  • Maintain your account security
  • Improve app performance and features

3 Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your household data is only shared with members of your household who you explicitly invite.

4 Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely on encrypted servers. We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, hashed passwords, and secure token-based authentication.

5 Your Rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access your data — Profile → Download my data
  • Delete your account and all data — Profile → Delete account
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account

6 Cookies & Tracking

Sivena does not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics. We do not serve ads or share data with advertisers.

7 Children's Privacy

Sivena is designed for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Child profiles within the app are managed by parent or guardian accounts.

8 Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes through the app.

9 Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or data requests, reach us at:
