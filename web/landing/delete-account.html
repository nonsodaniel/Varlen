Delete Your Account

Sivena — Family & Life Coordination App

1 Delete from inside the app

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data directly from the Sivena app:

  1. Open the Sivena app on your device.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom navigation bar.
  3. Tap the Settings icon (gear icon, top right).
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm when prompted.

Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted immediately.

2 Can't access the app?

If you no longer have access to the app, email us and we will delete your account manually within 7 days:

support@sivena.app

Include the email address associated with your account in your request.

3 What gets deleted

  • Your profile (name, email address, avatar)
  • All household data you created — tasks, events, expenses, journals, wellness entries, meal plans, memories, baby logs, goals, routines, lists, polls, timelines, habits
  • Your authentication credentials
  • Push notification tokens
  • Subscription entitlement records

4 What is retained

  • Anonymised crash and error logs held by Sentry for up to 90 days — these contain no personally identifiable information
  • Aggregated billing records held by RevenueCat as required by law
