What is Sivena?

Sivena is a global Life Coordination Platform designed to simplify everyday life by bringing people, responsibilities, memories, and planning into one beautifully connected experience.

Reduce the mental effort required to live life together.

## Mobile Experience Enhancement Ideas

### 1. Animated "Momentum" Home Screen
Replace the static Today view with an animated score/momentum meter — a glowing ring that fills up as users complete tasks, log meals, check off habits, and log wellness. The ring pulses when near 100% and explodes in confetti when complete. Makes daily progress feel like a game.

### 2. Relationship / Life Timeline
A beautiful vertical timeline (like a photo book) that auto-generates life milestones from app data — first shared task, budget goals hit, streaks reached, anniversary countdowns. Shareable as a card. Makes the app feel deeply personal and memorable.

### 3. "Pulse" — Live Household Activity Feed
A real-time activity ticker on the Today screen showing what household members are doing right now: "Alex just completed Morning Run ✓", "Jordan added 3 items to Grocery list". Creates a sense of shared presence and connection even when apart.

### 4. Smart Weekly Brief (Sunday Evening Push)
Every Sunday at 6pm, a rich push notification opens a beautiful "This Week" story card — streaks, best moments, money saved vs budgeted, mood trend, upcoming events. Swipeable slides, shareable. Think Spotify Wrapped but weekly.

### 5. "Quick Win" Micro-Tasks Tray
A swipeable horizontal tray just below the hero card showing 3–5 things that take under 2 minutes — log today's mood, confirm tomorrow's dinner, mark a habit done, settle a $5 split. Tapping one animates a satisfying check and the card flies away. Keeps engagement high with micro-interactions.
