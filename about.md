# Sivena - Life, Together

## What is Sivena?

Sivena is an all-in-one life management platform designed for individuals, couples, families, and parents who want to coordinate, organize, and thrive in their daily lives together. It is a mobile-first application (iOS & Android) backed by a robust cloud API, built to replace the fragmented mess of separate apps for tasks, calendars, budgets, meal planning, wellness tracking, and household coordination.

Sivena is not just another productivity app. It is a **relationship-aware life operating system** that adapts its entire interface, features, and intelligence based on how you live — whether you're managing life solo, sharing it with a partner, running a household, or navigating the beautiful chaos of parenting.

---

## The Problem Sivena Solves

Modern life involves juggling dozens of disconnected tools: a calendar here, a shared expense tracker there, a separate app for meal planning, another for habits, a notes app for shopping lists, spreadsheets for budgets. Couples text each other "did you pay the electricity bill?" Families lose track of who's picking up the kids. New parents can't remember when the last feeding was at 3 AM.

Sivena brings all of this into one beautifully designed, intelligent platform where everyone stays in sync — always.

## The Four Life Modes

At the heart of Sivena is the **Life Mode** system. When you first open the app, you choose how you live, and Sivena transforms to match:

### 1. Single Mode — "Just Me"
Your personal command center. Sivena becomes a focused self-management tool with emphasis on personal productivity, habits, wellness, goal tracking, and self-care. The dashboard shows *your* day, *your* tasks, *your* budget, *your* wellness.

### 2. Couple Mode — "Partner"
Designed for two people sharing a life. The app becomes relationship-aware: shared calendars, split expenses with settlement tracking, "Our Day" views showing both partners' schedules side-by-side, a shared memory lane, date ideas, relationship milestones, polls for making decisions together, and a "days together" counter. The interface uses warm pinks and romantic gradients.

### 3. Family Mode — "Family"
Built for multi-person households. The dashboard shows every family member's schedule, a chore board with assignments, family meal planning, household finances with per-member balance tracking, family activity feeds, collaborative polls, and shared timelines. The interface uses green tones representing growth and togetherness.

### 4. Parenting Mode — "Parenting"
Tailored for parents with young children. Includes everything from Family Mode plus baby tracking (feeding logs, sleep logs, diaper changes), growth milestones, bedtime countdowns, child activity tracking, and developmental insights. The interface uses warm amber tones.

## Core Features

### Tasks & Chores
- Create, assign, and track tasks across the household
- Task categories: Household, Errand, Personal, Work, Other
- Priority levels (high/low)
- Recurring task scheduling
- Assign tasks to specific household members
- Task completion tracking with progress indicators
- Chore rotation system for families

### Shared Calendar
- Unified calendar showing all household members' events
- Color-coded events per member
- All-day event support
- Event locations and notes
- Shared event creation (visible to all members)
- Per-member schedule views in family mode
- Calendar integration with tasks and countdowns

### Expenses & Budget Management
- Log expenses with categories and amounts
- Shared expense tracking (who paid, who benefits)
- Settlement system — calculates exactly who owes whom
- Monthly budget creation with spending limits per category
- Budget utilization tracking (percentage-based)
- Savings goals with progress visualization
- Expense insights and spending trend analytics
- Support for 12+ currencies (USD, EUR, GBP, NGN, CAD, AUD, JPY, INR, ZAR, BRL, KES, GHS)
- One-tap settle-up interface

### Meal Planning
- Plan breakfast, lunch, dinner, and snacks for every day
- Meal emoji, preparation time, and servings
- Weekly meal overview
- Meal suggestions connected to shopping lists
- Visible on the daily dashboard

### Wellness & Self-Care
- Daily mood tracking (5-level scale)
- Sleep hours logging
- Water intake tracking
- Optional journal notes per entry
- Mood trend visualization over time
- Sleep pattern analysis
- Wellness-based AI suggestions
- Streak tracking for consistent logging

### Habits & Routines
- Create daily or weekly habits with custom emojis
- Streak tracking (current and best streak)
- 7-day consistency visualization dots
- Frequency configuration
- Color-coded habit categories
- Progress banner showing daily completion
- Celebration animations when all habits are done
- Weekly consistency reporting

### Goals
- Set personal and household goals
- Target setting with measurable progress
- Category-based organization
- Progress percentage tracking
- Near-completion alerts
- Goal history and achievements

### Shopping Lists
- Create multiple named lists (Groceries, Household, etc.)
- Add/check/remove items
- Per-list completion progress
- Item count display
- Quick access from the home screen

### Journal
- Free-form journaling with date tracking
- Write, edit, and delete entries
- Personal reflection space
- Historical journal browsing

### Countdowns
- Create countdowns to important dates (birthdays, vacations, anniversaries)
- Days remaining display
- Featured on the home screen
- Urgency-based sorting

### Memories & Timeline
- Save special moments with titles, dates, and emojis
- Color-coded memory cards (8 tint options)
- Horizontally scrollable memory lane
- Shared family timeline
- Milestone recording for children
- Date-based chronological view

### Baby & Child Tracking (Parenting Mode)
- Log feedings: time, duration, type, notes
- Log sleep: start time, duration, quality
- Log diaper changes: time, notes
- Query logs by date range
- "Last log" display for quick reference
- Growth milestone tracking with dates
- Bedtime countdown timer
- Child profile management (name, DOB, color)

## AI Assistant — Sivena AI

Sivena includes an embedded AI assistant that understands your entire life context:

### What It Knows
- Your tasks, events, and calendar
- Your budget and spending patterns
- Your habits, streaks, and wellness data
- Your meal plans and shopping lists
- Your goals and progress
- Your household members and their activities
- Your life mode and relationship context

### What It Does
- Answers questions about your schedule, budget, and tasks
- Provides proactive suggestions (overdue tasks, budget warnings, wellness nudges)
- Offers personalized insights based on your data patterns
- Helps plan your week
- Suggests meals, activities, and habits
- Celebrates your achievements and streaks
- Understands relationship dynamics (references partner by name in couple mode, children in parenting mode)

### Rate Limiting
- 3 AI messages per day (free tier)
- Conversation history preserved across sessions
- Smart context building from live data
